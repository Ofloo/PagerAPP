PagerAPP — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-16

PagerAPP is a client for a PagerMCP server. This policy explains what the app does with your data. In short: PagerAPP does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

What the app stores on your device

PagerAPP stores the following locally, in the app's private storage, and nowhere else:

  • A mailbox UUID that identifies your mailbox on the PagerMCP server you configure.
  • Your settings: server URL, polling interval, per-status rules, active hours, and bypass/silence/watch preferences.
  • A local history of pages you have received, and a set of already-seen page IDs used to avoid showing duplicates.

None of this is uploaded anywhere by PagerAPP. You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Network connections

PagerAPP communicates only with the PagerMCP server address that you enter yourself, over the connection you configure (for example HTTPS). It uses that connection to create a mailbox, fetch queued pages, and — only when you tap ACK on a page that opted in — to send an acknowledgement back. It sends no other data, and no data to the developer or any third party.

Permissions

  • Internet — to reach your PagerMCP server.
  • Notifications — to show pages as notifications.
  • Camera — used only if you choose to scan a mailbox QR code; the image is processed on your device and is never uploaded.
  • Run at startup / foreground service — to keep polling for pages in the background and to resume polling after a reboot.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

PagerAPP contains no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking SDKs. There is no account, no sign-in, and no telemetry.

Children

PagerAPP is a developer/operations tool and is not directed at children.
